About

I've spent most of my career on high-traffic, customer-facing apps and the internal tools behind them: multi-step application flows, self-service portals, micro-frontend platforms and data-heavy dashboards. I care about the boring parts that make them good: fast pages, accessible UI, clear APIs and tests that catch things before customers do.

For the last couple of years most of my work has involved AI. That means connecting LLMs to real systems safely: retrieval over company documents, assistants that can take actions with the customer's confirmation, and the evaluation and guardrail work that gets a feature through security review. I've also done a lot of real-time work with WebSockets, Kafka and streaming responses, so AI features feel instant instead of bolted on.

I've led teams as the technical lead: setting coding standards, reviewing PRs, mentoring onshore and offshore developers, and owning CI/CD pipelines and production releases from build to sign-off.
